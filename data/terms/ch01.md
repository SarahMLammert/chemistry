## Chemistry
field: chemistry
section: 1.1

The study of matter: composition, structure, properties, and behavior.

## Hypothesis
field: chemistry
section: 1.1

A proposed explanation that can be tested.

## Scientific Theory
field: chemistry
section: 1.1

An explanation that has been tested and is well supported.

## Scientific Law
field: chemistry
section: 1.1

Describes what happens, without explaining why.

## Scientific Method
field: chemistry
section: 1.1

The process that connects observations, hypotheses, theories, and laws.

## Macroscopic Domain
field: chemistry
section: 1.1

Human/physical view.

## Microscopic Domain
field: chemistry
section: 1.1

Atom/microscope view.

## Symbolic Domain
field: chemistry
section: 1.1

Words/formulas/diagrams.

## Matter
field: chemistry
section: 1.2

Anything that has mass and volume.

## Solid
field: chemistry
section: 1.2

Definite shape and definite volume.

## Liquid
field: chemistry
section: 1.2

Variable shape, definite volume.

## Gas
field: chemistry
section: 1.2

Variable shape and variable volume.

## Plasma
field: chemistry
section: 1.2

A gas that contains charged particles.

## Mass
field: chemistry
section: 1.2

The amount of matter contained in an object. SI unit: kilogram (kg).

## Weight
field: chemistry
section: 1.2

Gravity's force on an object. SI unit: newton (N).

## Law of Conservation of Matter
field: chemistry
section: 1.2

Physical and chemical changes never change the amount of matter.
